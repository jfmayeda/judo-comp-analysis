- **White** (entry level)
- **Blue** (developing)
- **Brown** (proficient)

## Design Philosophy

### Threshold Progression
Most badges follow one of these progression patterns:

1. **Standard** (most technique/participation): 1 → 5 → 15 → 40
2. **Easier** (spirit/clutch): 1 → 3 → 8 → 20
3. **Harder** (grip/waza-ari): 3 → 10 → 25 → 60
4. **Medium** (renzoku): 1 → 5 → 12 → 30
5. **Gradual** (tokui-builder): 2 → 4 → 6 → 10
6. **Quick** (medals/full-card): 1 → 2 → 5 → 10/12

### White Belt (Entry)
Usually 1-3 occurrences - achievable early to build engagement

### Blue Belt (Developing)
Usually 3-10 occurrences - shows consistent practice/participation

### Brown Belt (Proficient)
Usually 8-25 occurrences - demonstrates solid skill development

### Black Belt (Mastered)
Usually 15-60 occurrences - represents true mastery over time

## Coach-Awarded Badges
The following badges require coach input and cannot be auto-calculated:
- Good Uke (partner work quality)
- Student of the Game (sportsmanship, listening, attitude)
- Team Spirit (supporting teammates)
- Quiet Confidence (composure under pressure)
- Escape Artist (noted escapes from difficult positions)

## Future Data Sources
To make badges fully automatic, we would need:
- **SmoothComp API**: Match results, ippons, waza-ari, tournament attendance
- **Coach Notes**: Manual awards for spirit badges
- **Practice Tracking**: Iron Streak (attendance)
- **Weigh-in System**: Made Weight, Mat Presence
- **Video Analysis**: Southpaw, Ambidex, Counter, Ashi detection
- **Opponent Notes DB**: Scout Ready completion tracking

## Privacy & Safety
- Only Demo A / Sample B athletes show badges in current implementation
- No real athlete data exposed
- First name + last initial only (no full names)
- No photos
- No public leaderboards or rankings
- Designed for coach/parent view, not peer comparison

## Visual Design
- Circular badge frames (Pokémon GO style)
- Rank border colors: White → Blue → Brown → Black
- Progress bars: Cyan (#06B6D4)
- Detail modal: Progress ring, tier row, category tag
- Responsive grid: 3-col mobile, 4-col tablet, 6-col desktop
